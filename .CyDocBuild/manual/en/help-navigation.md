## Open the right help {#local-reading}
Open `Documentation/Documentation.html` directly in your browser. The full reader, its images and diagrams are included with the application. JavaScript is required. Keep the complete Documentation folder together when moving the software.

The ReShade **User guide** button opens the introduction. **F1** while its inspector is focused opens the [inspector](doc:inspector), [capture](doc:capture), or [Timeline/Debug section](doc:inspector#timeline-and-debug) for the current tab. OBS opens [its reception guide](doc:obs). Unreal's **Help → CyGameCapture user guide** opens [Unreal help](doc:unreal); `CyGameCapture.Help fr` opens French help.

## Find information {#search}
Use the search field at the top or **Ctrl+K**. Type a task, setting or public API name. Results link to matching pages and sections; content and code-language filters narrow the search. Open the sidebar's disclosure arrows to explore subpages. Hover or focus a navigation item to read its complete title and description.

## Images and comparisons {#images}
Image controls appear on hover or keyboard focus and stay accessible on touch screens. Open media settings for zoom and annotation controls. Drag to pan, use the wheel or zoom buttons, and press **Escape** to reduce an enlarged image within this window. The A/B slider compares real captures taken at different animation moments.

[Inspect the annotated buffer list](doc:inspector?media=m1-buffer-inspector&note=buffer-list).

## Language and reading preferences {#language-and-theme}
English is the default and the complete French edition uses the same page and section addresses. Change language in the header. Choose a theme from the palette menu. The reader keeps reading positions, code tabs and navigation scroll during the session. Explicit section and media links take priority over saved positions.

My space contains bookmarks and notes. These personal data stay in this browser; export them if you need a backup. Persistence depends on the browser's file-origin storage policy, and moving the manual can change that storage context. Tab reaches controls; Enter or Space activates them.
